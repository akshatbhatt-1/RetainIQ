# Customer Personas

## Digital Explorers
Young, digitally engaged customers with lower balances but high platform interaction. Responsive to app-based incentives and gamified engagement.

## Wealthy Wanderers
High-balance customers with weaker engagement signals. Financially valuable but vulnerable to churn due to low ecosystem attachment.

## Loyal Core Customers
Long-tenure, highly engaged customers with stable behavioral patterns and lower churn probability.

## Silent Risk Customers
Low-engagement customers exhibiting behavioral disengagement and elevated churn tendencies.
